# Limud

A personal Mishnah and Rambam one-year plan. Open on an iPhone in Safari, then Share, Add to Home Screen.
Progress is saved on the device; use Settings, Backup to keep a copy.

## Days off

- **Busy today, push to tomorrow** on Today makes today a day off. Its learning moves to the days after it and the plan ends later. Shabbat stays the long day, because learning moves by hours.
- **Plan days off…** on Today adds one date or a range, past or future, and lists every day off with a Remove button.
- When you're behind, **Push the plan back instead** turns past days that are Not logged or Missed into days off. Partial days stay as they are.
- Days marked done are locked: skips and settings changes never change what they hold.
- Every change can be undone from the toast, or with "Day off · Undo" on that day.
